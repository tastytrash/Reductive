# Reductive

A Fabric mod for Minecraft that adds utility items and blocks, centred around refining vanilla minecraft materials into smaller parts.

# Items

### Tools & Weapons
- **Drill (Basic)**: A handheld drill for breaking blocks faster than a pickaxe, but can only break blocks a wooden pickaxe or shovel can.
- **Industrial Drill**: An upgraded drill that breaks in a 3x3 area.
- **Chainsaw (Basic)**: A chainsaw for cutting wood more efficiently than an axe.
- **Industrial Chainsaw**: An upgraded chainsaw that breaks multiple wood blocks.
- **Slingshot**: A weapon that fires pebbles, dynamite or fire charges. (supports crossbow enchantments)

### Utility
- **Matchstick**: A single-use flint and steel.
- **Lapis Bottle o' Enchanting**: Throw to gain 100 xp.
- **Portable Crafter**: A portable crafting table that works like a normal crafting grid.

### Materials & Resources
- **Pebble**: Smaller cobblestone.
- **Coal Chunk**: Smaller coal.
- **Lapis Lazuli Fragment**: Smaller lapis lazuli.
- **Quartz Shard**: Smaller quartz.
- **Diamond Shard**: Smaller diamond.
- **Netherite Nugget**: Smaller netherite.
- **Copper Coil**: An advanced crafting material.
- **Redstone Processor**: An advanced crafting material.
- **Lapis Infused Bottle**: A bottle for holding xp.

### Components
- **Drill Tips** (Iron, Gold, Diamond, Netherite): Tips for drills.
- **Drill Bodies** (Basic, Industrial): Bodies for drills.
- **Chainsaw Blades** (Iron, Gold, Diamond, Netherite): Blades for chainsaws.
- **Chainsaw Bodies** (Basic, Industrial): Bodies for chainsaws.
- **Engines** (Basic, Industrial): Mechanical components.

## Blocks

- **Experience Tank**: A storage block for experience, holds up to 1600 xp. Use a lapis or normal bottle to take 100/10 xp, normal Bottle o' Enchanting's now always grant 10 xp.

# Installation

1. Download or build the mod jar.
2. Place it in your Minecraft mods folder.
3. Launch Minecraft with Fabric and Fabric API installed.
